Analytics engineering/operational infrastructure

Supply Chain Control Tower

dbt · one model · one source of truth · one queue of what needs action

Built to stop chasing shipment status across disconnected systems, it became the visibility layer other departments started running on. Purchase orders, domestic and import and export shipments, and warehouse receiving modeled into one dimensional model, then surfaced as an exception queue: every shipment classified with the reason attached.
